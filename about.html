Kaushik Mitra Venkat

Robotics Engineer

I'm a Robotics Engineer who is constantly fascinated by a simple but deep question: can we truly understand how our intelligence works and build a machine that recreates it? Moving beyond just automating tasks and creating systems that can operate where humans physically shouldn't have to, handle what's unpredictable, and solve problems that actually matter.

A robotic system looks overwhelming until we decompose it. Then it becomes a collection of logical building blocks, each with a reason for existing. I like to understand each block, understand how they connect, and then build it. What's truly satisfying is seeing the system work in the real world, beyond simulations. That's where the real application begins.

What excites me most is what happens when AI meets classical robotics. Reinforcement learning, imitation learning, VLA models, not as replacements but as tools that push robots further into unstructured, real environments. Beyond the technology, I like to explore and understand more where robotics can create an impact, particularly in situations like safety-critical and mission-critical tasks, where a capable autonomous system doesn't just add efficiency, it changes what's possible.
